# ▶️ Simulation and Output

Run the solve and bring the result back onto your Blender meshes:
interactively from the sidebar, from JupyterLab, baked down to plain
keyframes, or written to a USD or Alembic cache.

- [Running the Simulation](simulating.md): **Transfer**, **Run** and
  **Fetch All Animation** on the **Solver** panel, the status and
  statistics shown during a solve, **Resume** and recovery.
- [JupyterLab](jupyterlab.md): drive a project from a notebook on the
  solver host, with or without Blender open.
- [Baking Animation](baking.md): convert the fetched animation into shape
  keys and keyframes that do not depend on the add-on.
- [Exporting USD and Alembic Caches](exporting.md): write the fetched
  animation to a point cache for another application and leave the scene
  as it is.
