# 🔒 Constraints

Shape the motion by pinning vertices, stitching groups together, or adding
parametric collision boundaries that never appear in the scene.

- [Pins and Operations](pins.md): hold vertices in place, or drive them with
  timed operations (**Move By**, **Spin**, **Scale**, **Torque**) or with
  motion you keyframe or capture.
- [Invisible Colliders](colliders.md): parametric walls and spheres with
  keyframed position and radius.
- [Snap and Merge](snap_merge.md): align one mesh to another and stitch the
  two across groups.

The motion of a **Static** collider mesh is not on these pages. It is covered
under Scene, in [Static Objects](../scene/static_objects.md).
